
Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

1. Scope of Service
The App is a guidance tool for university admissions. Important: The App facilitates the organization and tracking of applications; however, official admission is contingent upon the successful submission and verification of physical documents at the University Registrar.

2. Data Collection (DPA & GDPR Compliance)
To provide our services, we collect the following Personal Data:

Identity Data: Name, date of birth, and student identification numbers.

Contact Data: Email address and phone number for status notifications.

Academic Data: GPA, test scores, and previous school information for eligibility checks.

Digital Pre-checks: Images of documents uploaded for preliminary review.

3. Purpose of Processing
Your data is processed based on Contractual Necessity and Legitimate Interest:

To verify eligibility for specific academic programs.

To manage appointment scheduling for physical document drop-offs.

To provide real-time updates on your admission status.

4. Data Subject Rights
Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (PH) and GDPR (EU), you have the right to:

Access & Rectification: Request a copy of your data or correct inaccuracies.

Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of your account (subject to university record-keeping laws).

Data Portability: Request a digital copy of your provided data.

Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

5. Data Retention & Security
Storage: Digital copies uploaded for "pre-checks" are stored on secured servers with AES-256 encryption.

Retention: If an application is unsuccessful or withdrawn, personal data is purged after [e.g., 1 year], unless a longer period is required by the Education Act.

Physical Documents: The App does not "store" physical documents; once handed to the university, those are governed by the University’s Manual of Regulations.

6. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell your data. Data is only shared with:

Internal University Departments (Registrar, Dean’s Office, Accounting).

Cloud service providers (as data processors) under strict confidentiality agreements.

7. User Responsibilities
Users must provide truthful and accurate information.

Users acknowledge that the digital "pre-check" does not constitute final acceptance of a document; the physical copy remains the "Source of Truth."

Acceptance
By clicking "I Agree" or using the App, you signify your "Informed Consent" as defined by the DPA of 2012 and "Explicit Consent" under the GDPR.
