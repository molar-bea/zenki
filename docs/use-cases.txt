1. Profile Setup & Eligibility Check
Before diving into paperwork, the student needs to know what they actually qualify for.

User Action: Student enters their academic background (e.g., GPA, high school track, standardized test scores).

System Action: The app filters and suggests programs/courses the student is eligible to apply for.

Goal: Prevent students from wasting time on programs they don't meet the criteria for.

2. Interactive Document Checklist
Since students must submit physical copies, the app acts as their "digital folder."

User Action: Student selects a target program.

System Action: Generates a dynamic checklist (e.g., Birth Certificate, Form 138, Good Moral Character, 2x2 Photos).

Key Feature: Include a "Prepared" toggle for each item so students can track what they’ve already put in their physical envelope.

3. Step-by-Step Submission Guide
This is the "GPS" for the physical submission process.

User Action: Student views the "Submission Map."

System Action: Displays the specific office locations, operating hours, and the exact sequence of windows they need to visit (e.g., Registrar -> Accounting -> Dean’s Office).

Goal: Reduce anxiety by showing exactly where to walk once they arrive on campus with their documents.

4. Appointment Scheduling
To avoid the dreaded long lines, the MVP should manage flow.

User Action: Student selects a date and time slot to drop off their physical documents.

System Action: Issues a digital queue number or QR code.

Goal: Ensure the university staff isn't overwhelmed and the student doesn't wait for hours.

5. Status Tracker (Post-Submission)
Once the physical copies are handed over, the student usually enters a "black hole" of waiting.

User Action: Student checks the app for updates.

System Action: Updates the status from "Documents Submitted" to "Under Review" or "Verified."

Goal: Provide peace of mind and reduce the number of students calling the office for updates.
